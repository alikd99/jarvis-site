Jarvis

Jarvis: a personal desktop voice assistant used by its owner.

Jarvis runs on its owner's Windows PC and helps by voice and by chat. When the owner asks, it reads and sends email, checks the calendar and adds events, keeps a task list, sets reminders and saves notes. It is a personal tool, not a public service: there is no sign-up and no other users.
